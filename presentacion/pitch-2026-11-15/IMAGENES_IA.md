# Ilustraciones conceptuales generadas

Se utilizó la herramienta integrada image_gen, sin llamadas a DeepSeek. Las imágenes no representan misiones, pacientes ni resultados reales. Se guardaron en assets y se identifican como ilustraciones conceptuales en las diapositivas y las fuentes.

## contraste-espacio-tierra-ia.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: widescreen 16:9 keynote presentation background for TwinNauta, a student space health evidence project. Primary request: a cinematic conceptual diptych showing the human parallel between an astronaut in microgravity and an older adult resting in a hospital bed, WITHOUT implying the environments are physiologically identical. Composition: very wide panoramic 16:9, figures in the right two thirds with a generous quiet almost black navy left third for large editable text to be added later. Astronaut on the center-left within the image, calmly floating in a realistic dim orbital cabin, wearing a simple white space suit with no insignia, looking toward an older adult patient resting comfortably in a hospital bed on the right, awake and dignified, no distress. The two scenes transition subtly through a vertical band of cyan light, no surreal body merges. A small distant Earth through the cabin window, generic hospital architecture with no recognizable city. Style: premium science editorial conceptual illustration, restrained photographic cinematic rendering, human and empathetic, dramatic lighting, rich shadows, finely detailed fabrics and natural faces. Palette: deep midnight navy, cool desaturated whites, controlled electric cyan accents and subtle warm skin tones. Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermark, NO NASA insignia, NO fake medical UI, NO medical graph, NO glowing internal organs, NO implied cure or diagnostic output. Clearly a composed conceptual artwork rather than a documentary photograph. High visual impact, sophisticated, minimal clutter.

## portada-orbita-ia.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium widescreen 16:9 pitch cover background for a space-health research software student project. A solitary astronaut in a restrained white space suit and helmet on the far RIGHT of frame inside the shadowed window of an orbital spacecraft, the vast curved blue Earth in the lower right distance. Emphasize the human silhouette, isolation, wonder and responsibility, not sci-fi gadgets. Camera medium wide, astronaut from waist up in profile gazing toward Earth. Left 55 percent is beautiful near-black navy negative space with a subtle atmospheric blue fade, intentionally free for very large editorial typography added separately. High-end cinematic conceptual photography/illustration, sharp suit texture and subtle cyan rim light, realistic light and deep blacks, subtle stars. Powerful minimalist composition, no oversaturated glow, no holograms, no dashboards. Absolutely no text, no logos, no NASA insignia, no watermark, no charts, no lettering.
